FAQ

Common questions

Do I need my own AI infrastructure?

No, we typically integrate established AI providers through their APIs, so you don't need to run your own models.

Will the chatbot only answer from our content?

Yes, we design chatbots to stay grounded in the information you provide, rather than answering freely.

What tools can be automated together?

Most tools with an API or a supported integration — forms, spreadsheets, CRMs, email and messaging platforms among them.

Is this a fully autonomous AI system?

We design automation with appropriate checks, especially where accuracy matters, rather than leaving critical steps fully unsupervised.
