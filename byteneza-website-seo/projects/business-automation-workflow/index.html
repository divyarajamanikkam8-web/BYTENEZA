Business Automation Workflow for a Service Team

An automation workflow connecting a service business's intake form, spreadsheet tracking and email notifications.

Automation platform (n8n)Google Sheets APIEmail automation

Challenge

The team was manually copying new customer inquiries from a form into a spreadsheet and emailing follow-ups one by one.

Solution

We connected the intake form directly to their tracking sheet and set up automated follow-up emails triggered by new entries.

Features

  • Form-to-spreadsheet automation
  • Automated follow-up emails
  • Basic error notification if a step fails

Technology

n8n workflow automationGoogle Sheets APITransactional email service

Result

The manual copy-paste step was removed from the team's daily process, and follow-ups now go out automatically when a new inquiry arrives.
